FAQ

Trust comes from clarity. No fog. No games.

Is this legit?

Yes. Payments are through Stripe, which provides receipts and records. We also publish a clear scope: we provide navigation, screening, and application assistance — not brokerage representation.

Do you guarantee approval or housing?

No. Nobody legitimate can guarantee approval. We provide a clean process: verification steps, organization, and clear next actions.

Why do paid lanes exist?

Paid lanes cover time-consuming administrative processing and screening. They also filter out time-wasters so serious clients get priority.

What should I do first?

Start free. If you need a quick “is this listing legit?” check, use Listing Verification ($19). If you want priority processing and application review, choose the $75 lane.
