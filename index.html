FAQ

Trust is built by clarity. Here’s the truth, up front.

Is this a scam?

No. Payments (if you choose a paid lane) are handled by Stripe, which provides receipts and buyer protections. We also use written policies so nothing is “mystery meat.” You always know what you’re paying for: processing, screening, and administrative work.

Do you guarantee approval or housing?

No. Nobody legitimate can guarantee approval. What we do guarantee is a clean process: organization, scam avoidance, and strong application presentation so you move smarter and waste less time.

Are you a real estate broker or realtor?

No. We’re a housing navigation and application assistance service. We do not negotiate pricing, represent parties, or take commissions. We help you prepare, screen, and navigate.

Why is there a fee?

Because time is the real cost. Paid lanes cover administrative processing, screening, coordination, and verification guidance. This also filters out time-wasters so serious people get priority attention.
